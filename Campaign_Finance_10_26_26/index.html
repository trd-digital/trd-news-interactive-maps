About the data

Itemized federal contributions reported to the FEC by donors whose employer or name ties them to one of 85 real estate firms and trade groups, compiled by The Real Deal. The 2026 cycle runs through .

Party is the recipient's: candidate and party committees use their FEC party. PACs, super PACs and joint fundraising committees that back one party's candidates were classified by TRD. Trade, company and issue PACs, and smaller committees, are shown as nonpartisan & other.

Memo entries and ActBlue/WinRed pass-throughs are excluded, as are Realtor associations' transfers into NAR's own committees, so money isn't counted twice. Refunds are netted out. Donor names are as reported, so one person can appear under two spellings.

Source: Federal Election Commission data compiled by The Real Deal
